Use `parseWorktreeList` and `parseStatus` from `scripts/lib/repository_truth.cjs`. The CLI takes `entry|exit --manifest PATH --json`; manifest schema 1 has nonempty task_id and owner, absolute worktree_path, `refs/heads/...` branch, and 40-hex base_sha. The entry receipt records exact task/owner/path/branch/base/current HEAD, clean status, and UTC observation time. Exit consumes that receipt plus explicit validation commands/results and intended disposition `retain|merge-review|remove-review`; it emits current HEAD, clean status, `base_sha...HEAD` diff summary, validation, and disposition as separate facts. No receipt is cleanup authorization.
</interfaces>
