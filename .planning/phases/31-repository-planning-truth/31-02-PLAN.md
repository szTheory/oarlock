<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|---|---|
| Markdown/config/phase files → planning parser | Repository files may be malformed, contradictory, stale, oversized, symlinked, or changed during collection. |
| Installed GSD queries → repository evaluator | Runtime results are corroboration only and cannot supersede repository-local authorities. |
| Diagnostic repair proposal → maintainer/agent | Displayed commands or patches must remain inert data and must not be executed by the health process. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T-31-06 | Tampering / Elevation of Privilege | Authority resolver | high | mitigate | Encode datum-specific authorities, reject disagreements, and prove caches/directories cannot promote scope or completion. |
| T-31-07 | Repudiation | Completion validator | high | mitigate | Require the explicit roadmap-summary-verification/evidence-requirement proof chain with per-link diagnostic evidence. |
| T-31-08 | Tampering | Repair reporting | high | mitigate | Keep repair values as rendered data only; expose no apply/repair flag and assert before/after manifests. |
| T-31-09 | Denial of Service / Tampering | Concurrent planning reads | medium | mitigate | Bound reads and detect source identity changes, returning an incomplete-snapshot exit instead of mixed conclusions. |
| T-31-10 | Information Disclosure | Planning reference traversal | medium | mitigate | Normalize tracked in-repository paths, report escaping/broken targets, and do not follow arbitrary external links. |
</threat_model>
